## Same card, two machines

A Pi 5 and the ASUS A15 run the same closed row. Neither needs a network, a log table, or a step loop for that row.
The Pi keeps the gate and the closed row. The A15 keeps that row and can carry a remainder that has no closed ratio.
The GPU is not required for the closed row.
An API, if called, receives the outcomes and the stops. It does not receive a request to iterate.

## Radio

A subscribed radio may deliver a reading onto one channel.
It does not fold a peer's reading back into the Pi's row, and it does not average.
The closed row still finishes if the radio is down.
The peer receives the ratio and the stops.

## What a device is

USB, Bluetooth, and Wi-Fi deliver a reading. They are not extra formulas.
A device guides nothing until it is allowed onto one channel.
An unknown device is logged and stays silent.

Camera frame rate is a time. A pixel is a length only after one measured scale.
Accelerometer is acceleration, with its own noise floor and its own clip.
Magnetometer is a direction.
A packet time is a time. A missing interval is not divided. A late packet is not now.
Signal strength is not a meter until it is set against a measured length.
Bluetooth audio is a sample rate. It does not steer.
USB power and USB throughput are ceilings. Past them, the overrun stops or the rate drops.

## Live channels

Every subscribed machine: convert a length as a length, a time as a time, a frequency as a frequency. Outside that machine's band, stop.

Knee: torque into force, one root. Stroke inside its stops. Two tendon forces, not averaged.
The locked knee figures stay the example, not a new derivation: 180 to 250 Nm at 50 mm is 3.6 to 5.0 kN. Impact near 4120 N is checked against the 6 to 8 kN static band.
gamma stays off the link. The gel wall is not a joint limit.

Car: v^2 = 2 a s, one root. Gap over closing speed, and stop if that speed is zero.
Grip is the tire's cap, not c. An echo time becomes a range before it is compared.
A frame rate is not a distance.

Bore: advance is length over time. Penetration per turn is one ratio. Spoil for a fixed radius is face area times the shove.

Implant: tighter than the Pi. Ceilings are power, heat, and charge. The tighter stop wins.

Depth along a thread is a length, not a rate.

The insertion robot is a different subscription. It does not run the decoder, and the implant does not run the robot.

## How the channels run

One seated formula, one channel. Concurrent is not fused.
Wrong kind, or outside the band, is silence. Silence is not a vote.
When two live channels both speak, the tighter stop wins. They are not averaged.
Unseated rows do not get a channel. gamma, the pion length, and the Planck floor stay dark on the knee, the car, and the bore.
A spectrum, a scene, uncut ground, or a many-thread pattern has no formula channel. It goes up with the limits attached.
