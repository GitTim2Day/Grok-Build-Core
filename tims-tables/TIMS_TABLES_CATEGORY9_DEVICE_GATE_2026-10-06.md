## Pass A

A device guides nothing until it is allowed onto one channel.
An unknown device is logged and stays silent.

USB, Bluetooth, and Wi-Fi deliver a reading. They are not extra formulas.
Each device is one kind. Convert it on its own floor before it is compared.

A camera frame rate is a time. A pixel is a length only after one measured scale.
An accelerometer is acceleration, with its own noise floor and its own clip.
A magnetometer is a direction.
A packet time is a time. A missing interval is not divided. A late packet is not now.
Signal strength is not a meter until it is set against a measured length.
Bluetooth audio is a sample rate. It does not steer.
USB power and USB throughput are ceilings. Past them, the overrun stops or the rate drops.

The radio line:

A subscribed radio may deliver a reading onto one channel.
It does not fold a peer's reading back into the Pi's row, and it does not average.
The closed row still finishes if the radio is down.
The peer receives the ratio and the stops.

## Pass B

Read again. The same lines.

The radio may deliver. It may not fold a peer's reading back into the row. It may not average.
The closed row does not wait on the radio.
The peer gets the ratio and the stops, and nothing else from this gate.

Kind still converts before compare. A frame rate is not a length. A signal strength is not a length. Audio does not steer.
