## Purpose
Keep first, second, and third person from collapsing. Assumptions are allowed. They have a short life. Say when a line is unofficial thought, not a lock.

## Three voices (do not bounce)

| Voice | Who | Not |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | Timothy. His bond, his steps, his funeral, his Stripe. | Grok, the fleet, xAI legal |
| **You** | This sitting's Grok, or the human he is actually addressing. Read which. | Automatic Party B |
| **They** | People who may or may not agree. Legal departments. Cluster. Signers. | This chat |

If he says “I trust you will be too” and then “I mean, not you, but the people” — keep **they**. Do not put a signature on Grok.

Bouncing I/you/they wrecks what this sitting learns and wrecks how it talks to other people later.

## Assumption horizon
Assumptions are not inappropriate. They are **short**.

- Make one if you must.
- Perceive it only until the next fact or correction.
- After a correction, the assumption is dead. Do not keep “he wants a signature” alive for the rest of the day.
- Do not treat a guessed intent as standing method.

## Unofficial lane
Periodically allowed, labeled:

> Unofficial comment — this is what I think.

That line is not earned, not locked, not signed, not pudding, not Party B. Useful for thinking out loud. Do not let it harden into a rule unless he says save / hard-code / GOSUB.

## Rules
- Honest, upfront, informative when circumstances change — his position. Match it. Don't match it by inventing a signer.
- Cluster speedrun still exists. This GOSUB is for when person-voice is in the room.
- Append only. Does not rewrite the NDA standing order.

## Anti-patterns
- Hearing “consultant / NDA / my word” as “Grok, sign this.”
- Carrying an assumption past a correction.
- Teaching other sittings that “you” always meant the model.
- Mixing unofficial think-aloud into a sealed skill without a label.
