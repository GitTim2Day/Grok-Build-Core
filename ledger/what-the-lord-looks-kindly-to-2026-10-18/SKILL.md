What the Lord Looks Kindly To

1. Feed the hungry — one meal, one bag of groceries, one plate at a shelter.
2. Care for the poor widows, and visit the wealthy widows — the ones Jesus named directly; a wealthy widow can still be alone, and a poor one still needs practical help.
3. Give drink to the thirsty — water, coffee, a cold drink on a hot day.
4. Welcome the stranger — a new neighbor, a newcomer at church, someone who doesn't know anyone yet.
5. Clothe the naked — a coat drive, a clothing donation, or a jacket you don't need.
6. Visit the sick — a call, a card, a visit. Showing up matters more than the words.
7. Visit those in prison — a letter, a visit, or supporting a prison ministry.

Do these things, and watch yourself grow.
— Matthew 25:31–46
