# matthew-25-acts-of-mercy

Written 2026-10-18 for Timothy Norman's Grok Build project, with Timothy's
framing kept close to his words.

## The to-do list

Core acts (reference: Matthew 25:35-36):
1. Feed the hungry.
2. Give drink to the thirsty.
3. Welcome the stranger.
4. Clothe the naked.
5. Visit the sick.
6. Visit those in prison.

Timothy's addition:
7. Care for the widow and the orphan.
   This one is not in the Matthew 25:35-36 list. It is Timothy's addition,
   grounded elsewhere in scripture (reference: James 1:27). Cite it as a
   reference only; do not put quoted words in anyone's mouth.

## Timothy's framing

In Jesus' day widows and orphans were left to beg. Nowadays there are wealthy
widows and rarely wealthy orphans, but also poor ones. The categories are the
same, but the need isn't always visible. A wealthy widow can still be alone; a
poor one still needs practical help.

**Filter:** widows in need and orphans in need: all the orphans and most of
the widows.

## Cadence

- **One a week** is what most can hold.
- **One a day** for momentum.

## The point

It's not the size, it's the attempt. Attempting breaks the selfish habit.

"We'll always have the poor with us" (reference: Matthew 26:11) makes the list
all the more important, not less.

## How to use this skill
- When Timothy asks for a mercy plan, offer the checklist below for the week
  or the day, with the seven acts as the options.
- Keep it small and concrete: one named act, one person or place, one day.
- Do not judge size. A done small act counts; an attempt counts.
- Do not invent scripture quotes. Give the reference (book chapter:verse)
  and let Timothy read it in his own Bible.
- Do not contact anyone, sign up for anything, or spend on Timothy's behalf;
  the acts are his to do.
