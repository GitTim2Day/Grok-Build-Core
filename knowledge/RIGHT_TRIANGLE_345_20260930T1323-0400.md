Tim's form: the square root of a squared plus b squared equals the square root of c squared.
Both sides are the long side. The square root of c squared is c when c is a length.

Unit: divide the side you have by its ratio number. That is 1.
Multiply that 1 by each missing ratio number.
You have the missing side before any square or square root.
