# BLOOD_HOMEOSTASIS_2026-11-26

Blood as a closed loop with inputs and outputs at every turn.

## Inputs
- Minerals: sodium, potassium, calcium, magnesium, chloride, phosphate, iron, zinc, copper, selenium
- Vitamins: B12, D, folate, A, E, K, C, B-complex
- Enzymes and coenzymes: thousands of catalytic proteins, NADH, FADH2, CoA, TPP, PLP
- Plasma proteins: albumin, globulins, fibrinogen, transferrin
- Glucose, lipids, hormones, gases (O2, CO2), water

## Outputs
- Renal: water, urea, creatinine, electrolytes, drugs, toxins
- Fecal: unabsorbed nutrients, bile pigments, bacteria, fiber residue
- Respiratory: CO2, water vapor
- Sweat: water, sodium, trace minerals
- Cellular uptake: glucose, amino acids, fatty acids, oxygen

## Homeostasis Mechanisms
- Kidneys: filtration, reabsorption, secretion, erythropoietin, renin-angiotensin
- Liver: synthesis, storage, detoxification, bile production
- Endocrine: insulin, glucagon, thyroid, adrenal, parathyroid
- Buffers: bicarbonate, phosphate, protein
- Bone: calcium and phosphate reservoir
- Spleen: red blood cell recycling, iron storage

## Monitoring Markers
- Electrolytes: Na, K, Cl, Ca, Mg, PO4
- Renal: eGFR, creatinine, BUN
- Glucose: fasting, HbA1c
- CBC: RBC, hemoglobin, hematocrit, WBC, platelets
- Lipids: total, LDL, HDL, triglycerides
- Liver: ALT, AST, bilirubin, albumin
- Vitamins: B12, D, folate
- Iron: ferritin, TIBC, transferrin saturation
- Thyroid: TSH, free T4
- Inflammation: CRP

## Replenishment Strategy
- Food first: whole foods before supplements
- Targeted gaps: only what monitoring shows missing
- Renal-adjusted: eGFR 61 requires dose adjustment for renally cleared drugs
- Non-statin lipid paths: PCSK9 inhibitors, low-dose colchicine given statin intolerance (rhabdomyolysis risk)

## Geometry Tie-Back
Node-and-edge architecture: Krebs cycle (metabolite nodes, enzyme edges) → token lattice (SVCT cells, radial prongs) → organ diagrams (vessel junctions, capillary gates) → machine control (sensor-actuator leaves). Same pattern across scales: discrete contact points, directional flow, energy conserved at each handoff, failure isolated to one leaf.
