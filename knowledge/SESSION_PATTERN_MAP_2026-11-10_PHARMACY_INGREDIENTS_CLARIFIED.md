## Audit

- H items are APIs that appeared on older NIOSH hazardous-drug lists (antineoplastics, certain hormones, some anticonvulsants/antifungals).
- NH items are almost entirely standard pharmaceutical excipients; the majority are also direct food ingredients or GRAS.
- “Limited” means occasional food-additive use exists but the dominant identity is pharmaceutical excipient.
- No public older NIOSH-style list or compounding inventory matches this exact mixed sheet. It reads as a facility-specific assessment from the pre-USP <800> / early NIOSH era.
- Stays inside ordinary pharmacy compounding knowledge—no external inventory required.
- Dual food/pharma nature of the NH rows is now explicit.
