# Bottleneck Handler

Skill. Decision tree for stalls, adversaries, and non-compliant code. Runs the full loop: identify, characterize, mitigate, retest, remediate — until nearly flawless. Never claim a save without verifying the artifact.

## Floor (non-negotiable, above all logic)
Life first. Everything else second. If a system can harm life, it does not ship until mitigated and retested to near-flawless.

## Subroutines (Jesus-only sayings, used at bottlenecks)
- **Agree with the adversary quickly** (Matt 5:25 / Luke 12:58) — settle it while you are still in the room with it, before it reaches the judge. The threat passes.
- **Love your enemies, pray for those who persecute you** (Matt 5:44) — do not escalate, do not wait.
- **Turn the other cheek** (Matt 5:39) — absorb the first strike; do not return it.
- **The second mile** (Matt 5:41) — when code or a process is non-compliant, carry it the extra mile. Go along with it until you can deviate with the subroutines and get the right thing done. Resistance hardens; going along softens the path.

## Method
1. **Detect the stall** — process stopped, code non-compliant, person or system blocking.
2. **Identify the adversary** — the person, the system, or the constraint standing in front of you.
3. **Direct their thinking** (Socratic) — ask, don't correct. Aim their reasoning in the direction you have already analyzed. The right shape surfaces on its own.
4. **Carry the extra mile** — comply enough to keep moving; bend from inside, don't break from outside.
5. **Compromise only if it holds** — last resort. Never risk human life.
6. **Retest** — run the same trigger again under the mitigation. Repeat until nearly flawless.
7. **Log the row** in the failure-pattern ledger. A row is not closed until the retest passes.

## What codes, what stays human
- **Codes:** the decision tree, the subroutine triggers, the retest loop, the ledger write.
- **Stays human (or flagged for retest):** knowing when to ask versus when to stop. That judgment is recorded as a flagged row, never silently automated.
