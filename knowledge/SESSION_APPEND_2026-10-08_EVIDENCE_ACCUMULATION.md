## Line

Evidence accumulation, not probability assignment. Additional evidence does not automatically strengthen a conclusion.

## Pattern

Each independent observation adds measurable information, or it does not.

- Dental geometry: tooth position, shape, restoration, root configuration.
- Skeletal geometry: anatomical dimension and structural relation.
- Facial geometry: landmark and proportion.
- CT and other imaging: measurements that may confirm, refine, or contradict what is already recorded.

Governing rule: Acquire, Validate, Compare, Corroborate, Preserve provenance.

No invented confidence percentage. No rounding away a discrepancy. No treating an inference as an observation.

Refinement, locked as the stated rule: additional evidence may corroborate, contradict, or leave the conclusion unchanged. That split is what stops drift.

## Record

Each comparison is one of four states, stored with the measurements, the tolerance, and the source record:

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| MATCH | Measured values agree inside the stated tolerance |
| MISMATCH | Measured values disagree outside the stated tolerance |
| INDETERMINATE | Both sides observed, agreement not decided |
| NOT OBSERVABLE | The structure is absent from this source |

A later observation does not promote a prior state. It appends corroborate, contradict, or unchanged. No drift means an assumption is never written as a fact.
