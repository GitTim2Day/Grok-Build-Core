## Layman version

What we did
We refused to mash three different experiments into one headline number.

The three clocks
- Oscillation experiments measure gaps between neutrino masses, not the smallest mass.
- KATRIN measures whether the electron leftover energy is missing a chunk bigger than about half an electron-volt. So far: no.
- Galaxy surveys (DESI) plus leftover Big Bang light guess the sum of the three masses — only after you pick a dark-energy model and whether the three masses are equal.

The simple arithmetic
If the lightest neutrino were almost zero, the other two already add up to about 59 thousandths of an electron-volt.
The EV2 idea adds a tiny extra piece, about 0.3 of those same units.
KATRIN’s ruler is marked in 450 of those units. It cannot see 0.3.
One popular DESI + standard-cosmology cap sits near 64. Another way of quoting the same survey goes down to 53, which is below the 59 that oscillation already requires. Change the dark-energy assumption and the cap jumps to 163.

Conclusion you can take or leave
The fight in the journals is not “did EV2 predict the neutrino mass.”
The fight is “is a galaxy-survey cap allowed to go under the floor that oscillation already set.”
EV2’s extra 0.3 is a passenger on that sum. Anyone who rounds 53, 59, and 64 into one “almost measured” number is doing the thing this method forbids.

The principle, in one line
A published limit is a ceiling for that machine. It is not a measurement of a different quantity, and it is not permission to delete a smaller token.

## College-student version

Claim under test
EV2 predicts m1 = π × 10^-4 eV ≈ 0.314 meV and therefore Σ ≈ 58.5 meV using older PDG-style m2, m3.
Question: does 2026 published data confirm, refute, or fail to resolve that claim once each dataset is kept in its own variable?

Logic rules
1. Name the observable. Δm², m_β, and Σ are not interchangeable.
2. Name the prior. A DESI bound under ΛCDM + degenerate masses is not the same object as the same data under w0wa.
3. Truncate the comparison to the instrument floor. Do not raise a theory token to that floor.
4. A negative best-fit mass-squared or a negative Σ posterior is an analysis face, not a physical negative mass.
5. Correlation is not derivation. A 283 meV sterile guess is off this table until mixing is specified.

Rebuilt numbers (NuFIT 6.0 NO with SK; truncate toward zero on reported digits)

- Oscillation floor, m1 = 0: Σ ≥ 58.784 meV.
- EV2 m1 plus current Δm²: Σ ≈ 59.105 meV. Increment from the node ≈ 0.32 meV.
- EV2 paper as printed (m3 = 49.53 meV): Σ = 58.521 meV. Difference vs NuFIT rebuild is mostly vintage Δm², not the node.
- Inverted-ordering floor: Σ ≥ 100.4 meV.

Published comparison faces
- KATRIN: m_β < 0.45 eV (90% CL). Increment 0.32 meV is unresolved.
- DESI DR2 + CMB, ΛCDM, degenerate: Σ < 0.0642 eV (95%). Room vs 59.1 meV ~ 5 meV.
- Same program, Feldman–Cousins after the zero boundary: Σ < 0.053 eV. Undercuts the oscillation floor. Tension is oscillation vs cosmology, not EV2 vs KATRIN.
- Same data, dynamical dark energy: Σ < 0.163 eV. Window reopens.
- Some combinations prefer an unphysical negative Σ. Treat as model stress (lensing / DE), not a mass.

Conclusions (reject any you can break)
C1. EV2 is not currently confirmed or refuted by KATRIN. Wrong observable, wrong scale.
C2. EV2 does not predict the cosmological sum in a way 2026 Σ meters can award. It predicts a sub-meV add-on. The sum is dominated by oscillation Δm².
C3. If a reader accepts the DESI+ΛCDM+FC 53 meV cap as physical, they must already fight oscillation, with or without EV2.
C4. If a reader accepts only the 64 meV ΛCDM cap, EV2 and the NH floor both fit; inverted ordering does not. That hierarchy cut is the survey’s, not EV2’s.
C5. Do not fuse gates. A single public number (“neutrino mass ≈ 0.06 eV”) is a different epistemology.

What would change C2
A direct m1 or m_β measurement at a few tenths of a meV, or a Σ measurement with a named prior tight to ≪ 0.3 meV around 59 meV. Neither exists today.

What would not
Another decimal of π on a KATRIN slide. Another sterile mass without |U|. Averaging 53 and 64 and calling it confirmation.
