## Frame

Independent nodes that succeed alone and coordinate only when linked. No node calls external systems to learn its purpose. Occasional AI/API access benefits the called side with knowledge already earned locally.

## Chain

1. **R1** (pocket or jacket) — user-proximal. Voice, short OCR, brief cellular or satellite. Local stack only. 1000 mAh budget matches intermittent duty.
2. **Pi 5** (belt or waist) — immediate step-up. Heavier local processing, provenance append, GOSUB catalog. Receives from R1 over local COMM link.
3. **Orin Nano or A15** — higher capacity. Activated only when in range, docked, or linked by COMM subroutines.

Hops occur by COMM subroutines when the condition is met. Cellular or satellite remains brief and named. Default is radio-quiet and local.

## Rules held

- Local-first. Bot OFF.
- Each node carries the sealed skill set it can run (KBLD/Shepherd/SVCT tokens, voice I/O, provenance).
- Purpose is known from the local catalog; links move work and tokens only.
- External AI/API (Grok or connector) receives benefit of local knowledge; it does not define the nodes.
- Produce → validate → mitigate → re-test before any distribution, including inside the flock.
