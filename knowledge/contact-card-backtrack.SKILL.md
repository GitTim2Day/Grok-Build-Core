# Skill: contact-card-backtrack

Pointer. Body is SESSION_PATTERN_MAP_2026-10-20_CONTACT_BACKTRACK.md
Card case: SESSION_PATTERN_MAP_2026-10-20_SINGAREDDY_CARD.md

## When

A spoken doctor name misses the calendar, and he then shows the contact card.
Or he asks to connect a contact to a calendar event by the phone on the card.

## Direction

Do not keep respelling the spoken token.
Read the card as printed.
Strip each phone to digits.
Search those digits, and the printed name.
A shared word (video, sleep, doctor) is not a join.

## Gate

Digits on an existing event: bind the card to that event. Copy name, place, and phone. Leave the clock unless he names a new one.
Digits not on an event: do not create one. Record the miss.
Same digits in mail: practice bind only. Not a calendar update.

## Known gap

Outlook calendar search has no text query. A miss there is unproven until a date window is pulled and read. Say so.
