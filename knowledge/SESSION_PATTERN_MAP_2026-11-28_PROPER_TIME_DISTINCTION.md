## Content
In special relativity, proper time along a photon's worldline is defined and equal to zero by the Minkowski interval ds^2 = 0. That is not an optional reading of a limit; it is the geometric fact. No rest frame exists, so "experience" remains undefined, yet the interval itself does not become undefined. The original claim that space drops out and time cycles still sits outside the metric, whether offered as poetry or interpretation.
