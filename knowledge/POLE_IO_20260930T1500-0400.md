# Poles are ports
Owner: Tim
Name: pole-io-20260930T1500-0400
Said: 2026-09-30T15:00:00-04:00

The poles are for ingress and egress only. Input and output.
The north pole (0, 0, 13u) reflects to (0, 0, -13u). They are not the same point.
The equator is fixed. A point there equals its reflection, so it cannot be both a port and a mirror.
A joint stays on the 5u ring or on a square face. Not on a pole.
The 64 longitudes stay open. No cell index.
