## What was offered
How different pipes **evolve** and stay **braided** (not fused):

| Pipe | Job | Not the same as |
|---|---|---|
| Capillary bed | Exchange. Thin wall. | A tube that decides waste vs keep |
| Glomerulus | Specialized capillary tuft. Filter. | The rest of the nephron |
| Bowman's capsule | Catchment around the tuft. First filtrate lands here. | The tubule that edits the filtrate |
| PCT / loop / DCT / collecting duct | Variable permeability. Keep water, dump waste. | Venous blood |
| Vasa recta / peritubular capillaries | Return of what was reclaimed. | Urine |
| Synovium / synovial fluid | Another barrier + filtrate + local product (hyaluronan). Joint working fluid. | Kidney. Same *motif*, different organ |

Motif: wall with changing permeability, a filtrate, a return path. Two circuits. Intertwined like a braid — one organ, several pipes, none collapsed into one.
