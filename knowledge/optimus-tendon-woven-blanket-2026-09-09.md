# Optimus tendon woven blanket — 2026-09-09

Appended from conversation. Still sketch, not spec. Origin: Optimus tendon work.

## Construction
- Braided Dyneema sub-braids (12-strand per filament, same as capture tethers) woven into a flat blanket.
- Low mass: fabric-level areal density.
- Thermal: every fourth strand carries a copper element for low-resistance resistive heating — cold mitigation without a separate heater layer.
- Protection: weave acts as a shield. Strength below Kevlar but described as "pretty darn strong"; usable as lightweight coating or multi-layer shield.
